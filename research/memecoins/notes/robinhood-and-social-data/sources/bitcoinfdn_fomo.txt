What Does Fomo Mean in Crypto?
FOMO means fear of missing out, a familiar crypto trader emotion as prices move rapidly. The Fomo app takes its name from the familiar acronym.

Fomo combines token discovery, social activity, portfolio tracking, and crypto trading within a single pane. Users can explore assets, follow traders, receive alerts, and execute trades. Portfolio tools tie these activities together in the same interface.
Is Fomo a Crypto Exchange or Social Trading App?
Fomo is more in line with a social crypto trading application compared to a traditional centralized exchange. The interface is driven around feeds, traders, trends, and executing token trades directly.
The Fomo trading app is aimed at faster-discovery-focused users, and those who benefit from simplified multichain trading. Fomo’s social aspect may appeal to active traders following memecoins, new tokens, and general community activity.
How Does the Fomo App Work?
Social Trading and the Fomo Feed
The feed displays activity from traders and accounts a user chooses to follow. This makes the Fomo social trading application akin to a market-focused social network, with trading positioned alongside the content.
How the Fomo Leaderboard Works
The Fomo app leaderboard ranks traders based on performance data over set timelines. A user can open a profile and review results, instead of basing decisions on screenshots of the same information.
How Fomo Tracks Top Crypto Traders
Fomo ties public account activity to portfolio and trading statistics within the platform. Trader profiles can then display, or provide insight into, performance, activity, holdings, and other visible metrics.
Fomo Trading Alerts and Notifications
Users can enable Fomo app alerts for traders they follow. Notifications can bring attention to purchases and sales, but a user must elect to execute a trade.
How Fomo Supports Multichain Trading
Fomo routes supported assets across several blockchains, while positioning them in a shared interface. This eliminates the need for manual switching and eases cross-chain discovery. The unified interface removes much of the usual bridging friction.
How Gasless Trading Works on Fomo
Fomo abstracts, or sponsors, gas for supported trading flows. Deposits, withdrawals, or external transfers to the Fomo account may still involve network fees.

What Can You Trade on Fomo?
Current Fomo app supported chains include Solana, Base, BNB ▲ $723.92 Chain, Ethereum, Monad, and Robinhood Chain. Availability can vary, and users should verify with the current chain selector.
Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Other Tokens
Fomo offers major crypto assets, as well as smaller ones where chains are supported. Bitcoin exposure may take the form of a tradable product, although Bitcoin mainnet is not one of Fomo’s listed execution chains.
Memecoins and Trending Tokens on Fomo
The Fomo app memecoins experience is largely focused on fast-moving and viral assets. Trending sections, feeds, and trader activity can support discovery of these attention-seeking tokens.
How to Find New Tokens Before They Trend
Users can review trending lists and verified sections, trader feeds, and search tools. Early discovery carries high risk due to limited volume and liquidity, and trending status is no guarantee of token quality.

How to Create a Fomo Account
Open the Fomo app or web platform and sign in via a supported method. The onboarding process creates an embedded wallet, without requiring the traditional wallet setup first.
H3: How to Fund Your Fomo Account
Funding options can include USDC ▼ $0.9998 transfers and supported fiat payment methods such as Apple Pay or cards. Always complete the appropriate blockchain selection before sending crypto. A wrong network can be unrecoverable.
Search for a token, open the trading page, and enter an amount. Review the quoted price, fee, price impact, and network details before confirming the purchase.
How to Sell Crypto on Fomo
Open an existing position and select the sell option. Check the quoted output and costs before approving the transaction.
How to Track Your Portfolio and PnL
The Fomo app portfolio incorporates positions across supported networks. PnL data allows users to identify profitable and losing positions, without manually viewing several wallets.
How to View Your Trading History
Trading history records completed activity associated with the account. Reviewing these entries can allow a user to compare execution prices, timing, and realized results.
How to Withdraw Funds From Fomo
Open the appropriate wallet or cash section, select the withdrawal option, and enter a compatible destination address. Confirm the appropriate network and review charges before sending funds.
How to Find and Follow Crypto Traders on Fomo
How to Read the Fomo Leaderboard
Focus on the timeframe, rather than the headline rank. Short-term leaders may have dramatically different results than traders with longer activity.
How to Analyze a Trader’s Performance
Review a realized PnL, number of trades, holding behavior, and recent activity where available. One profitable trade should not drive a follow decision. Compare recent activity to longer periods before following any strategy.
How to Follow a Trader and Receive Alerts
Open a trader’s profile and select the follow function. Enable notifications if you wish to receive alerts when that account executes a visible trade.
Does Fomo Have Copy Trading?
Fomo does not rely on copy trading as its core social feature. Users can view activity and receive alerts, but must select whether to execute their own trades.
Fomo App Fees and Trading Costs
Does Fomo Charge Trading Fees?
Yes, trading can incorporate a Fomo platform fee. Spot pricing can vary by transaction, and the confirmation screen displays the applicable cost before execution. Price impact and spreads can incorporate additional trading costs.
How Fomo Handles Network and Gas Fees
Fomo promotes gasless trading for supported in-app flows by abstracting network gas. Separate blockchain costs can apply when funds move into and out of the platform.
Are Deposits and Withdrawals Free?
Crypto deposits may not have a Fomo platform charge, but the sending network can incur gas. Withdrawals can also involve network costs, while fiat providers may have separate fees.
How Fomo Fees Compare With Traditional Crypto Exchanges
Traditional exchanges often have set maker and taker schedules. Fomo emphasizes simplified execution, and traders should compare the final quoted cost, rather than headline rates.

Fomo App Security and Wallets
How Fomo Stores and Protects Your Crypto
The Fomo app wallet uses an embedded wallet model, designed to simplify self-custody. Account security therefore relies on the wallet infrastructure and login protection. Device security is vital as access controls determine the trading interface.
Does Fomo Use a Self-Custodial Wallet?
Fomo cites its wallet model as self-custodial or non-custodial. Users retain wallet control, but the interface removes much of the usual setup friction.
Does Fomo Require a Seed Phrase?
Normal onboarding does not require a user to write down a seed phrase. Recovery is tied to supported account methods, while wallet export options may provide additional control.
Fomo KYC and Account Verification
Basic crypto access may not have the same onboarding as a traditional exchange account. Fiat payment partners or specific products can require identity verification.
What Happens If You Lose Access to Your Account?
Recovery depends on the login method linked to the embedded wallet. Users should make sure emails, device access, biometrics, and any available wallet export credentials are secure.

What Makes Fomo Different From Other Crypto Trading Apps?
Fomo puts trader discovery directly alongside execution, rather than using a separate tool. This is the key point in any balanced Fomo app review.
Is the Fomo App Safe to Use?
Fomo combines embedded wallet infrastructure and account authentication, as well as device-level protections. Security still relies on users protecting their login credentials and checking addresses carefully. No wallet design can eliminate token, market, or smart-contract risk.
Risks of Trading Memecoins and Trending Tokens
Memecoins can experience rapid price movements due to liquidity and sentiment. Small tokens can have risks related to smart-contract, spoofing, concentration, and exit liquidity.
Risks of Following Top Crypto Traders
A leaderboard displays past performance, not future returns. Traders may enter earlier, use different position sizes, and exit before a follower acts.
What to Check Before Making a Trade
Check the token contract, network liquidity, quoted price, fees, and position size. Also confirm that a trending ticker is the intended asset, rather than a lookalike token.
Fomo App vs Traditional Crypto Exchanges
Coinbase has a greater focus on traditional exchange services, fiat access, and established assets. Fomo is focused on discovering tokens through social data, while positioning multichain trading. Products serve differing trading habits, despite some overlap.
Binance offers a wider traditional exchange ecosystem, with order books and numerous financial products. Fomo uses a simplified social-first interface and discovery-focused approach.
